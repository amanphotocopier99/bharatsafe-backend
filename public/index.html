Nope. A phone camera reads the QR code and opens your secure BharatSafe profile in the browser.

They see the contact details you chose to share and can reach you without seeing your private account information.

Can I move my tag to a new thing? +

Yes. You can update the name and details on your profile anytime. The code stays the same.

Will it survive rain and sun? +

BharatSafe stickers are made with durable, weatherproof vinyl and a UV-resistant finish.
