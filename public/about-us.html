About BharatSafe

We build for the moment you hope never comes.

BharatSafe makes it possible for a stranger to help you — even before you can help yourself.

OUR STORY(01 — 05)

It starts with
what happens next.

It usually starts the same way. An accident on the highway. A bike parked the wrong way, blocking a gate. A suitcase that goes missing on a connecting flight. A dog that slips out during the Diwali fireworks.

In every one of these moments, the emergency itself often isn't the hardest part — it's the minutes that follow, when someone standing right there wants to help but has no way to reach you, your family, or anyone who can act.

BharatSafe started with that exact problem. We asked a simple question: what if a stranger could help, instantly, without needing an app, a login, or a phone call to the police first?

That question became a sticker — a small, waterproof QR code that carries your most important information, ready to be scanned by anyone, anywhere, in seconds.

Today, BharatSafe protects cars, bikes, luggage, and pets across India — quietly doing its job so that when something goes wrong, help isn't far behind.

WHY WE EXIST(02 — 05)

Make help
possible.

India has some of the busiest roads, airports, and streets in the world — and some of the strongest communities looking out for each other. What's often missing is the bridge between the two: a way for a willing stranger to actually do something useful in the first few minutes of an emergency.

We built BharatSafe to be that bridge. Not a tracking device. Not another app to install. Just a scan — simple enough for anyone to use, and private enough that you decide exactly what it shares.

Our Mission
To make every Indian road, ride, and journey a little safer — one scan at a time.

Our Vision
An India where no emergency goes unanswered simply because help couldn't find you in time.

WHAT WE STAND FOR(03 — 05)

Safety First

We design for the “what if” moments — the ones you hope never happen, but plan for anyway.

Radical Simplicity

No apps. No account for the person scanning. No friction between a stranger wanting to help and actually helping.

Privacy by Design

Your information is yours. You choose what's visible, you can update it anytime, and nothing is shared beyond what you've allowed.

Made for Bharat

Built and tested for Indian roads, Indian weather, and the everyday reality of commuting, travelling, and living here.

HOW WE GOT HERE(04 — 05)

Built from
lived moments.

BharatSafe is built by a small team of engineers, designers, and everyday commuters — people who've sat in traffic, filed lost-luggage reports, and once spent a frantic hour looking for a runaway pet.

We didn't set out to build a “product.” We set out to solve a problem we'd all lived through ourselves.

To make every Indian road, ride, and journey a little safer — one scan at a time.
LOOKING AHEAD(05 — 05)

Wherever care
could go missing.

We're just getting started. From cars and bikes to luggage and pets, our goal is simple: wherever something you care about could go missing or be caught in an emergency, BharatSafe should be there — one scan away from getting help.

Because in an emergency, the fastest connection is the one that matters most.
