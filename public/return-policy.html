RETURNS & REFUNDS

How returns
work.

  • Return window: Requests must be made within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Condition: The product must be unused, meaning the sticker or tag has not been applied, scanned, or activated, and it is in its original packaging.
  • How to request a return: Email bharatsafe.official@gmail.com within 48 hours with your order number and, if the return is for a damaged or wrong item, a photo of the product.
  • Refunds: Once we receive and check the returned product, your refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Return shipping: We arrange pickup for change-of-mind returns. If we sent a damaged, defective, or wrong item, we will cover the return shipping.

Not eligible for return

  • Products that have been used, applied to a surface, or activated
  • Returns requested after 48 hours of delivery
  • Products damaged by the customer after delivery
